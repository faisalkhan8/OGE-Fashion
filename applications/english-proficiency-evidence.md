## 1. Existing University of Sargodha evidence

The applicant's official University of Sargodha Registrar / Academic Branch Medium of Instruction letter:
- date: 17 December 2024;
- reference: SU/Acad/2370;
- certifies that the medium of instruction and examination at undergraduate, graduate and postgraduate level is English.

The University of Sargodha also publishes its Undergraduate Semester Regulations 2023. Rule 4.4 states that the medium of instruction and examination is English, except language courses and compulsory Islamic Studies, which may be offered in English or Urdu. Those regulations were approved for implementation from Fall 2023.

Official university sources:
- Rules and Regulations page: https://su.edu.pk/Rules-and-Regulations
- Undergraduate Semester Regulations 2023 PDF: https://su.edu.pk/upload/rti/Undergraduate%20Semester%20Regulations%202023.pdf

**Practical implication:** the Registrar's MOI letter is the primary individual evidence. For any university that requires the entire programme to have been taught exclusively in English, it is safer to request an additional programme-specific letter confirming the actual language used for all courses and examinations in the applicant's BS Software Engineering programme, including any general-education or compulsory language/Islamic Studies courses. Do not state that every course was taught exclusively in English unless that is accurate.

## 2. Stanford University — Computer Science PhD

Official pages:
- Graduate Admissions eligibility: https://gradadmissions.stanford.edu/apply/eligibility
- Graduate Admissions FAQ: https://gradadmissions.stanford.edu/apply/faq
- CS PhD FAQ: https://www.cs.stanford.edu/admissions/phd-admissions-frequently-asked-questions
- CS PhD application checklist: https://www.cs.stanford.edu/admissions/graduate-application-checklists

Stanford's central policy states that TOEFL/IELTS results are not required if an applicant has earned an international equivalent of a US Bachelor's degree from a university where all instruction was conducted in English. The CS PhD FAQ lists the same exception. The Graduate Admissions FAQ says that, if the applicant selects this route in the application, test scores are not required at application time; if admitted and the offer is accepted, the university may later request official documentation that the programme was taught exclusively in English.

A second possible route is at least two consecutive years of full-time professional or educational experience in English within the last ten years. Stanford's FAQ requires at least 24 consecutive months and that the relevant experience was conducted in English. If using work experience, the employer/supervisor may be asked to verify it after admission.

**Applicant evidence to assemble:**
- BS degree certificate and official transcript.
- Official UOS MOI letter (Ref. SU/Acad/2370, 17 December 2024).
- If relying on the degree route, a programme-specific Registrar/Department statement confirming that the entire BS Software Engineering programme was taught and examined exclusively in English.
- If relying on the work-experience route, the Priv Inc employment confirmation showing dates and full-time hours, plus supervisor verification of the English working language, if requested.

**Decision:** Stanford is a plausible no-test route, but the phrase "all instruction" / "exclusively in English" is strict. The University's general MOI letter supports the case; the applicant must ensure it accurately describes the actual course-by-course medium. A full-time English-work-experience route may provide an additional basis only if the applicant confirms that requirement is met.

## 3. Cornell University — Computer Science PhD

Official pages:
- Graduate School English proficiency requirement: https://gradschool.cornell.edu/admissions/application-steps/required-tests/english-language-proficiency-requirement-2/
- CS PhD application requirements: https://www.cs.cornell.edu/phd-computer-science/apply

Cornell Graduate School has a standing exemption for an applicant who will hold an international equivalent of a US Bachelor's degree from a university located in a country where English is an official language and English is the language of instruction. Pakistan is explicitly included among the examples. The medium of instruction must be shown on the transcript or easily verified on the university website, and the applicant must have been physically present in the exempt country for the majority of enrolment.

The exemption is requested inside the graduate application; Cornell says it does not pre-approve standing exemption requests by email. Research-degree applicants using this exemption may still need an English Language Assessment before serving as a teaching assistant.

The Cornell CS page contains a general statement that TOEFL/IELTS is required for international applicants. Read that together with the Graduate School's published standing exemptions: the standing-exemption route is the official mechanism by which a qualifying international applicant can meet the Graduate School rule without a test. It is not a general MOI-only waiver for every country.

**Applicant evidence to assemble:**
- BS degree certificate and official transcript.
- Official UOS MOI letter.
- UOS official regulations page/PDF showing the university's English-medium rule.
- Accurate confirmation that the applicant was physically present in Pakistan for the majority of the BS enrolment.

**Decision:** Cornell has the clearest published Pakistan-specific standing-exemption pathway. Eligibility still depends on the international degree-equivalency assessment, English-medium requirement, and physical-presence condition. Do not claim the exemption unless all conditions are true.

## 3. Northwestern University — Technology and Social Behavior PhD

Official programme page: https://tsb.northwestern.edu/admissions/apply.html

The TSB programme's published admissions instructions list as one English-proficiency route an official transcript verifying an undergraduate degree from an accredited four-year institution or equivalent where the language of instruction is English. This may fit the University of Sargodha BS and MOI documents, subject to programme and Graduate School review.

The programme separately states that international applicants appointed as teaching assistants must provide either a Test of Spoken English (TSE) score of at least 50 or a TOEFL iBT speaking score of at least 26. This is a teaching appointment requirement, not the same as the English-medium admissions route. If no speaking score is available, ask the programme whether an RA/fellowship funding path is possible; do not assume a TA appointment will be available.

The programme's Fall 2027 deadline is December 1, 2026. Its admissions page asks for an Academic Statement, Personal Statement, CV, transcripts and 2–3 recommendation letters. It notes that most entrants hold a master's degree or equivalent experience, but exceptional candidates may be admitted directly after a bachelor's degree.

**Decision:** plausible application route without IELTS/TOEFL through the published English-medium degree pathway, but written admission confirmation and TA/funding rules must be distinguished. The uploaded UOS MOI letter is relevant evidence; it does not itself settle the funding appointment requirements.

## 4. Recommended submission procedure

1. Upload the university's official transcript and degree certificate.
2. Upload the official UOS MOI letter.
3. Include the public UOS Regulations 2023 link where supplementary evidence is allowed.
4. For Stanford, only select the degree-based exemption if the full programme was in fact taught exclusively in English; request a programme-specific confirmation if necessary.
5. For Cornell, select the standing exemption only if Pakistan physical-presence and English-instruction conditions are met.
6. Retain the employment confirmation as supplementary evidence for Stanford's consecutive English-work route, but do not use that route unless the employer can truthfully verify the full-time English working environment.
7. If a portal or programme administrator says a test is required, follow the current written instruction rather than relying on this note.
