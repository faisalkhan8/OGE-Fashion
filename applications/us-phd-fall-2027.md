## Northwestern Technology and Social Behavior

- Deadline: **1 December 2026**.
- The programme combines Computer Science and Communication.
- The FAQ says a Master's degree is not required to apply. The admissions profile says many entrants hold a Master's or equivalent experience, although exceptional candidates may be admitted directly after a Bachelor's.
- Application: Academic Statement, Personal Statement, CV, transcripts and 2–3 recommendation letters.
- English route: official transcript verifying a four-year undergraduate degree at an institution where English is the language of instruction; upload UOS transcript and MOI for review.
- International applicants appointed as TAs need TSE 50 or TOEFL iBT speaking 26 (5 on the updated scale). This is separate from the MOI admission route. Ask whether an RA or fellowship appointment is possible without a qualifying speaking score.
- Funding: the TSB FAQ says admitted students receive five years of support through research assistantships, teaching assistantships or fellowships. Request aid in the application; there is no separate financial-aid application.

## Stanford Computer Science

- Deadline: **8 December 2026**.
- GRE is not required or considered for the CS PhD.
- Statement of Purpose: two pages maximum under the current CS checklist.
- Exactly three recommendation letters; at least two academic recommenders are recommended.
- English degree exemption requires an international-equivalent Bachelor's from a university where **all instruction** was conducted in English. UOS regulations state that English is the medium except language and Islamic Studies courses may be offered in English or Urdu. Request UOS confirmation of the actual medium of every course before claiming the exemption.
- Alternative: 24 or more consecutive months of full-time professional or educational experience conducted entirely in English within the last 10 years. Priv Inc's current employment confirmation proves role, dates and hours, but not the language of all work; it is not enough by itself to establish this route.
- Funding details and conditions should be confirmed in the programme offer.

## Cornell Computer Science

- Fall deadline: **15 December 2026**; verify the year-specific Fall 2027 portal before final submission.
- Direct PhD entry with a Bachelor's is permitted.
- Two recommendation letters are required; three or four are allowed.
- Statement of Purpose: concise, identify research interests and relevant faculty.
- Personal Statement: maximum 1,000 words.
- Pakistan is explicitly included in Cornell's standing exemption for an international-equivalent Bachelor's from a university in a country where English is official and English is the language of instruction. The applicant must have been physically present in Pakistan for most of enrolment; medium of instruction must appear on transcript or be easily verified on the university website.
- Research-degree applicants using this exemption may still need an English Language Assessment before TA appointment.
- Cornell CS says PhD students in good standing receive tuition, health insurance and a stipend.

Do not enter anyone's name or email until they agree. Stanford requires exactly three; Northwestern asks for 2–3; Cornell requires two and accepts up to four. Send each referee the CV, transcript, relevant statement, research brief and exact deadlines.
