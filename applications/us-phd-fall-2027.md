## Northwestern Technology and Social Behavior (TSB)

- Deadline: **1 December 2026**.
- The programme is interdisciplinary across Computer Science and Communication.
- It expects most students to hold a master's degree or equivalent experience, but exceptional candidates may be admitted directly after a bachelor's degree.
- Application: Academic Statement, Personal Statement, CV, transcripts and 2–3 recommendation letters.
- The programme accepts an official transcript verifying a four-year undergraduate degree from an institution where English is the language of instruction as one English route; upload the UOS MOI letter and transcript for review.
- International applicants appointed as TAs need TSE 50 or TOEFL iBT speaking 26 (5 on the updated scale). Admission via an English-medium degree does not automatically satisfy this appointment requirement. Ask the programme whether RA or fellowship support is possible without a speaking score.
- TSB says it generally admits PhD applicants only if financial support is available; no separate financial-aid application is required.

Official pages:
- https://tsb.northwestern.edu/admissions/apply.html
- https://tsb.northwestern.edu/admissions/financial-support.html

## Stanford Computer Science

- Deadline: **8 December 2026**.
- GRE is not required or considered for the CS PhD.
- Statement of Purpose: two pages maximum and 8,000 characters under the current CS FAQ.
- Three recommendation letters; at least two academic recommenders are recommended.
- The University English exemption requires an international-equivalent Bachelor's from a university where **all instruction** was conducted in English. The current UOS regulations state English is the medium except language and Islamic Studies courses may be taught in English or Urdu. Ask UOS for a programme-specific confirmation of the actual medium of every course before claiming this exemption.
- Alternative: 24 or more consecutive months of full-time professional or educational experience conducted entirely in English within the last 10 years. Priv Inc's current employment letter confirms employment and hours but not language of work; it is not enough by itself to establish this route.
- CS funding is provided under university/department terms; check the current funding guarantee and conditions before accepting any offer.

## Cornell Computer Science

- Target deadline: **15 December 2026**; the current department webpage still displays a Fall 2026 cycle deadline, so check the live Fall 2027 portal.
- The CS PhD accepts applicants with a Bachelor's degree directly to the PhD.
- The current departmental page requires **two recommendation letters; three or four are allowed**.
- Statement of Purpose: concise, identify research interests and relevant faculty.
- Personal Statement: maximum 1,000 words.
- Cornell's standing exemption includes Pakistan for applicants with an international-equivalent Bachelor's from a Pakistani university where English is the language of instruction. The applicant must have been physically present in Pakistan for most of enrolment; MOI must appear on transcript or be verifiable on the university website.
- Research-degree applicants using the standing exemption may still need an English Language Assessment before TA appointment.
- The CS department states full support for PhD students in good standing.

## Recommender strategy

- ideally two University of Sargodha faculty members who can assess your academic ability, capstone work and research potential;
- one further academic referee or professional supervisor who can provide specific evidence of technical judgement, independent work and collaboration.

Do not enter a recommender's name or email until they agree. Send each referee your CV, transcript, the relevant programme statement, the research discussion brief and the exact submission deadline.
