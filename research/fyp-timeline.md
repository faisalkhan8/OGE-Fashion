# Historical FYP period and project provenance

## Academic period

The original Final Year Project (FYP) belongs to the **7th and 8th semesters**, covering **November 2023 through May 2024**. This is the historical period to use when describing the university FYP, its original objectives, and work completed as part of the degree.

Do not backdate later research, code, experiments, or repository activity into this period. In particular, the expanded OGE-Fashion research framework and the GitHub work created in November 2026 are later developments.

## Original project context

The earlier FYP work focused on ontology-supported recommendations for clothing and makeup, with face-shape-related information as part of the original concept. The historical project discussions identify oval, diamond, and square face-shape categories and describe ontology work using Protégé/OWL.

These notes preserve the known project direction; they are not a substitute for the original submitted report, source files, supervisor records, or semester assessment documents. Exact milestone dates, implemented features, evaluation results, and authorship contributions should only be stated where the original artefacts or records support them.

## Later research extension

The current repository, **OGE-Fashion — An Ontology-Guided Explainable Recommendation Framework for Personalised Fashion and Beauty Recommendations**, is a later research extension. Its broader scope includes explicit user preferences, shared fashion/makeup concepts, evidence provenance, explainable recommendations, and comparative evaluation of content-based, ontology-only, ML-only, and hybrid approaches.

The later extension may reuse the original FYP motivation, but its new design decisions and implementation must be described using their actual dates. It must not be presented as completed during November 2023–May 2024.

## Reporting rule

For FYP reports or semester descriptions:
- Use **November 2023–May 2024** for the original 7th/8th-semester FYP period.
- Distinguish planned work from implemented work.
- Do not invent weekly milestones, performance metrics, deployment status, or evaluation outcomes.
- Describe post-May 2024 changes separately as later work or research extension.
