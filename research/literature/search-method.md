# Literature Search Method — OGE-Fashion
**Snapshot date:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Preliminary scoping method; not a registered or completed systematic review.

## Research areas
1. Fashion product, outfit, and compatibility recommendation.
2. Explainable fashion recommendation and compatibility assessment.
3. Makeup-look, makeup-product, and broader cosmetic recommendation (kept distinct).
4. Ontology-based and knowledge-based recommender systems.
5. Hybrid/neuro-symbolic recommendation integrating explicit knowledge and ML.
6. Context-aware and preference-constrained recommendation explanations.

## Sources/portals consulted in initial scoping
Publisher and official research pages from ACM-related records, CVF Open Access, AAAI, Frontiers, SpringerLink, institutional research portals, PubMed, and W3C. Search-engine results were used as discovery aids; citation facts were retained only where an identifiable publisher, venue, institutional or standards record was available.

## Proposed full review procedure
1. Translate RQ1–RQ4 into inclusion concepts and synonyms.
2. Search ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, Scopus/Web of Science (if institutionally accessible), SpringerLink, ScienceDirect, PubMed for relevant cosmetic/skin-care studies, and major conference proceedings.
3. Record database, exact query, date, result count, export file, and filters for every search.
4. Deduplicate using DOI/title.
5. Screen title/abstract against written inclusion/exclusion criteria.
6. Record full-text availability and reasons for exclusion.
7. Extract task, domain, knowledge representation, model, data, labels, evaluation, explanation method, limitations, and licence/data access information.
8. Map included studies to RQ1–RQ4 and evidence claims.
9. Keep fashion, makeup-look, and skincare/product recommendation as distinct tasks unless a paper explicitly links them.
10. Reassess the provisional research gap after screening.

## Inclusion criteria (proposed)
- Peer-reviewed research, established conference papers, authoritative surveys, and relevant standards.
- Direct contribution to one or more OGE-Fashion research areas.
- Sufficient bibliographic metadata to identify the source.
- Methods/data/evaluation described in an accessible abstract or full text.

## Exclusion criteria (proposed)
- Marketing/blog content without methodological evidence.
- Unverifiable or incomplete bibliographic records.
- Sources unrelated to recommendation, representation, explanation, or data governance.
- Duplicate reports of the same work (retain the most authoritative version and record related versions).

## Quality and extraction fields
- Bibliographic metadata and publication status.
- Study type and domain.
- Research objective and task formulation.
- Ontology/knowledge representation, if any.
- ML/recommendation architecture.
- Data source, sample and label details.
- Evaluation design and metrics.
- Explanation method and explanation evaluation.
- Bias, privacy, and data licence limitations.
- Relevance to each RQ and how directly the evidence supports it.

## Reporting boundary
The current literature evidence matrix is a targeted first pass. Do not call it a systematic review, claim completeness, or use it alone to establish absence of prior work.
